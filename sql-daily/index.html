        How to play
        
    Every day is a new feature: a movie-themed trilogy of SQL questions. Part I is easy, Part II is medium and Part III is hard. They come one at a time.
    
        
      
          
    • You get one minute per question. The clock starts when you click Begin, and each next part starts as soon as you move on. When time runs out, the question is over.
      • 
          
    • Run your query as often as you like to see its output. When you think it's right, Submit it. Wrong submissions don't cost points.
      • 
          
    • The game runs SQLite, so use LIMIT (not TOP) and strftime() for dates.
